# SEO growth for utility sites

## Input
- Public tool page list, intended users, geographic/language scope and performance/traffic evidence.
- Authorized Search Console, analytics or keyword research data if available.

## Procedure
1. Segment real user problems and search intent, including alternatives and competitive landscape; label research evidence and dates.
2. Evaluate whether each tool solves a distinct problem; recommend useful guidance, samples, troubleshooting and accessibility rather than mass-generated thin pages.
3. Plan descriptive URLs, unique headings, meta titles/descriptions, canonical, sitemap/robots and internal navigation; respect the framework's rendering approach.
4. Ensure tool content/primary UX does not rely on ads and does not misrepresent competitor/third-party features or permissions.
5. Record baseline for impressions, indexed pages, organic clicks, engagement and Core Web Vitals when actually measured; otherwise mark UNKNOWN.
6. Prioritize small experiments with explicit hypotheses and success/stop rules; do not optimize solely for ad views at the expense of utility.
7. Review policy, data privacy and consent before adding tracking scripts or launching promotional channels.

## Output
- Evidence-backed acquisition backlog and `docs/monetization/site-monetization-plan.md` updates.
- Per-route priorities, hypothesis, owner and outcome measure.

## Constraints
- No invented search volumes, guaranteed rankings, fabricated backlinks or click/traffic bots.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, doorway pages, cloaking, scraping private sources and duplicate auto-generated content.
- Research without authorized retrieval is unverified and must be explicitly marked pending.
